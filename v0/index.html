Company Profile and Services

Barak Strategy and Entrepreneurship Ltd. is a consulting, market development and investment company that specializes in mentoring entrepreneurs and new technologies in the fields of health, medical devices, biotechnology and foodtech. With an extensive clinical, scientific and business background and a network of contacts in product development, business development, market entry strategies, creating a sales force and management

 

We can accompany your idea and your company from the establishment stage to standing on its own, through :

  • product development
  • regulatory approval
  • market launch
  • Increase In Sales And Profitability
  • Providing-Investing Funds And Services For Initial Development And Operations
  • Assistance In Substantial Fundraising Activities And Strategic Alliances

Our executives enjoy a track record of successful product launches, investment rounds and M&A transactions.
